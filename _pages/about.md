I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, advised by [Xiang Ren](https://shanzhenren.github.io/). My research develops methods to diagnose and improve language-model reasoning, spanning reasoning diagnostics, post-training, agent routing and evaluation, and understanding how pretraining data shapes model behavior.

I am seeking full-time **Research Scientist** and **Applied Scientist** roles starting in 2027. I expect to graduate by May 2027.

## Background

At USC, I am a member of the [INK Lab](https://inklab.usc.edu/), and part of my work has been supported by an Amazon ML PhD Fellowship. Previously, I completed an M.S.E. in Computer Science at Princeton University, where I worked with [Danqi Chen](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~danqic/) and was named a Siebel Scholar, and a B.A. in Computer Science and Cognitive & Linguistic Sciences at [Wellesley College](https://www.wellesley.edu/). In high school, I represented China twice at the [International Linguistics Olympiad](https://ioling.org/).

Outside research, I enjoy sports, cooking and baking, reading, and playing tennis, and I wrote a personal [crash course](/self-physical-therapy-empowering-recovery/) on understanding and managing common bodily pain, based on years of physical therapy visits.
