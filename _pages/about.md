I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, advised by [Xiang Ren](https://shanzhenren.github.io/). During my PhD, I was awarded the [Amazon ML Fellowship](https://trustedai.usc.edu/20242025-amazon-ml-fellows). Previously, I completed an M.S.E. in Computer Science at Princeton University, where I worked with [Danqi Chen](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~danqic/) and was named a [Siebel Scholar](https://engineering.princeton.edu/news/2021/09/28/graduate-students-receive-siebel-scholar-awards), and a B.A. in Computer Science and Cognitive & Linguistic Sciences at [Wellesley College](https://www.wellesley.edu/).

My research develops methods to diagnose and improve language-model reasoning, spanning [reasoning diagnostics](#arbor), [post-training](#meta), [agent routing and evaluation](#adobe), and [understanding how pretraining data shapes model behavior](#culturegen-attr).

Along the way, I have released open-source tools and datasets: diagnostic tools for long reasoning traces ([ARBOR](https://github.com/INK-USC/ARBOR), [STIM](https://github.com/INK-USC/STIM)), pipelines that trace generations back to the pretraining documents that shaped them ([CULTURE-GEN](https://github.com/huihanlhh/Culture-Gen), [MEMOed](https://github.com/huihanlhh/CultureGenAttr)), and evaluation resources for long-tail knowledge ([LINK](https://github.com/INK-USC/LINK)) and conversational QA ([EvalConvQA](https://github.com/princeton-nlp/evalconvqa)).

I am seeking full-time **Research Scientist** and **Applied Scientist** roles starting in 2027. I expect to graduate by May 2027.
